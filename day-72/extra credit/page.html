A bachelor's degree is a great investment in your future, and one of the most common
                                degrees earned by college students. Some degrees pay you back, but others don't do much
                                to increase your earning potential. Your course of study influences your earning
                                potential, so in order to plan your financial future, use PayScale's College Salary
                                Report to rank how different majors for bachelor's degrees compare based on alumni
                                salaries.

                            
In today's job market, having a bachelor's degree is nearly a necessity to land a
                                rewarding, well-paying job with any possibility for advancement; a bachelor's degree is
                                almost essential for entry into most professional careers. In certain lines of work,
                                like teaching and engineering, you cannot enter the field without holding at least a
                                bachelor's degree.
